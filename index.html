Free travel guides for solo budget travelers

Tuk Or Walk is a free budget travel guide for people who travel alone and watch every baht. Every trip is walked on foot, then written up with the exact prices, the real walking time between stops, where the toilets and water are, and what I'd skip. Start in Thailand, with Sri Lanka, Laos and India on the way.

Exact prices

Transport fares, room deposits, a 40฿ ($1.10) bowl of noodles. Every cost in local currency and USD, so you can budget before you book.

Real walking times

Measured between stops on foot, not guessed from a map, so a full day costs you almost nothing in transport.

Honest negatives

What's small, what's broken, what I'd skip. The things other sites leave out, so your budget goes where it's worth it.

04 Budget travel questions About the site →

What is Tuk Or Walk?

A free, one-person budget travel guide for solo travelers. Every trip is walked on foot and written up with exact prices, real walking times and honest notes, with no sponsored posts.

Is Tuk Or Walk free?

Yes. Every guide is free to read. The site has no ads and no sponsored posts; some links may be affiliate links, explained on the affiliate disclosure page.

How much does a day of budget travel cost?

Each guide lists the real total. For example, one full day on a Bangkok walking route cost about 700฿ (roughly US$20), including a 500฿ ($14) room, a 40฿ ($1.10) bowl of noodles and the BTS fare, with no entrance fees.

Are the guides good for solo travelers?

Yes. They are written by a solo traveler for solo travelers: costs per person, routes you can walk alone, where to eat, where the toilets and water are, and what to skip.

Which countries are covered?

Thailand is live, with Sri Lanka, Laos and India on the way. More Asian countries will be added as they are walked.

How are walking times measured?

Each leg is timed on foot at a normal pace on the day of the walk, rather than estimated from a map.

Does Tuk Or Walk take sponsorship or gifted stays?

No. There are no sponsored posts, paid placements or gifted stays. See the editorial policy.

About Tuk Or Walk

Tuk Or Walk is a free budget travel guide for solo travelers, written by one person on foot. Before this site, I was already documenting trips on Google Maps, where my photo contributions have 7.3 million views. This is the long-form version: same honesty, more detail, no sponsored fluff.

Every guide covers the exact hotel, the exact price, the real time it takes to walk between stops, and the parts most blogs leave out because a sponsor wouldn't like them.

If you want the polished version, there are a hundred sites for that. This one is for the traveler who wants to know what the room is really like, whether there's anywhere to eat nearby, and whether ฿500 is a good deal or not.
