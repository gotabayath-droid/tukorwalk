What is in the Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 gallery?

50 original photos from the festival on the Chao Phraya River in Bangkok: the Wat Arun drone show (Thai cultural symbols, colors, hearts and the Thai flag), laser and stream shows, fireworks near Memorial Bridge and Wat Kalayanamit, and a roadside night photo spot.

Can I use these photos?

You can share the gallery link freely, including its preview image. For blogs, press or commercial use, ask first and credit "Photo: Tuk Or Walk". See the photo and image rights page.

I appear in a photo. Can it be removed?

Yes. Photos were taken at a public festival; email hello@tukorwalk.com and the photo will be removed promptly.
