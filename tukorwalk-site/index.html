About Tuk Or Walk

Tuk Or Walk is a free budget travel guide for solo travelers, written by one person on foot. Before this site, I was already documenting trips on Google Maps, where my photo contributions have 7.3 million views. This is the long-form version: same honesty, more detail, no sponsored fluff.

Every guide covers the exact hotel, the exact price, the real time it takes to walk between stops, and the parts most blogs leave out because a sponsor wouldn't like them.
