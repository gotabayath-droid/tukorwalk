Usually replies within a day

What you can email about

  • Corrections — a price changed, a place closed, a walking time was wrong.
  • Questions about a specific trip — hotel, food, route.
  • Press or collaboration.
  • Just saying hi — those are welcome too.

To help me answer quickly

A short, specific email gets a faster, more useful reply. If you're writing about one of these, include:

  • For corrections: the page URL, the line that's wrong, and what's correct now — a source or a photo helps.
  • For trip questions: the trip name and the stop. Screenshots welcome.
  • For press: the outlet, the deadline, and the angle in the first paragraph.
  • For anything else: just write. I read everything.

Other ways to reach me

For Maps-specific questions — a photo of mine, a review, a place I've pinned — the fastest reply is on the Google Maps contribution page. Same person, different inbox. The email above still works for everything else.

What you won't find here

No contact form that goes nowhere. No ticket queue that auto-closes. No chatbot that asks you to rephrase. No "we'll get back to you within 5–7 business days." Just a real person with a real inbox, and a real attempt to reply.
