This site collects as little as possible. Here's the complete list:

What we collect

  • Nothing, automatically. No analytics cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting.
  • If you email us (subscribe, correction, question), we keep your email address only to reply or send the new-trip notification you asked for. No sharing, no marketing lists.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads typefaces — your browser contacts Google to fetch font files. No cookies are set by this site for that.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site and its photos (R2). Standard server logs (IP, user agent) exist for security and are not used for tracking.
  • Google Maps links take you to maps.google.com, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Email hello@tukorwalk.com any time to ask what we hold about you, or to be deleted. There's nothing else to opt out of — there are no trackers to decline.
