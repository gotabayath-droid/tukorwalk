Good to know before you set off

What trips are on Tuk Or Walk?

A free Bangkok temples-and-shrines walk is live. Walks in Laos, Sri Lanka and India are being written up now, and more Asian countries will follow.

How much does a walk cost?

Each guide shows the real total. The live Bangkok walk cost about 700฿ (roughly US$20) for the whole day, including a 500฿ room, a 40฿ bowl of noodles and the BTS fare, with no entrance fees.

Are the walks free to follow?

Yes. Every guide is free to read, and every stop on the Bangkok walk was free to enter.

How are walking times measured?

Each leg is timed on foot at a normal pace on the day of the walk, not estimated from a map.

Can I do these walks alone?

Yes. The guides are written by a solo traveler for solo travelers: routes you can walk alone, costs per person, where to eat, toilets and water, and what to skip.

How do I find out when a new trip goes live?

Use the email box at the bottom of this page. You get one email when a new trip is published, with no marketing and no sharing.
