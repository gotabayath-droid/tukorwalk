Quick answer

The Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 festival runs from 13 November to 13 December 2026 along the Chao Phraya River in Bangkok, and it is free. A drone show lights the sky above Wat Arun at 7:45 PM, and fireworks follow at 8:45 PM near Memorial Bridge. The easiest route is the MRT to Sanam Chai station (Exit 1), then a walk of about 500 meters to the Wat Pho Pier opposite Wat Arun. Arrive by 6:00 PM for a good spot.

About the Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 festival

Thailand is full of festivals and spectacular shows at this time of year, from Christmas trees lighting up shopping malls to riverside events. One of the best is the Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 festival, a free riverside celebration in Bangkok that runs from 13 November to 13 December 2026. Its centerpiece is a drone show above Wat Arun, the Temple of Dawn, right over the Chao Phraya River, followed by fireworks further along the river.

Both shows are free. They draw large crowds of locals and international visitors, and the evening is easy to combine with a walk through the Tha Tien riverside area.

Getting there: MRT Sanam Chai to the Wat Pho Pier

The festival is easy to reach by public transport:

  1. Take the MRT to Sanam Chai station.
  2. Leave by Exit 1, near Museum Siam.
  3. Walk around to the back entrance of the museum.
  4. Turn right and continue straight for about 500 meters.

The walk ends at the Wat Pho Pier, directly opposite Wat Arun. This is the prime viewing location for the drone show.

Map: Wat Pho Pier (Tha Tien), opposite Wat Arun. Open the walking route from Sanam Chai in Google Maps.

The Wat Arun drone show

The drone show starts at 7:45 PM and lights up the night sky above the river with stunning detail. Hundreds of drones move in precise formations over Wat Arun, and the temple stays lit in the foreground.

Thai cultural symbols in the sky

The drones form characters and symbols from Thai culture: Hanuman, a yaksha guardian, the soaring Garuda and a warrior crossing the sky.

Colors, hearts and the Thai flag

The colors are vibrant and the formations precise: a galaxy pattern, teal and golden floral designs, a red heart and a heart in the colors of the Thai flag.

Laser and stream shows

Laser and water-stream effects are combined with the drones, and the river adds a second show in its reflections.

The drone finale

The show closes with a finale of smoke trails, a fireworks burst and fireworks and drones lighting the sky together.

Show schedule

The official schedule poster lists the show dates and times. The drone show starts at 7:45 PM and the fireworks at 8:45 PM.

The fireworks near Memorial Bridge

A spectacular fireworks show starts at 8:45 PM for anyone who walks further towards Memorial Bridge. Bursts of crimson, three-at-once trios, crackling riverfront shells and willow displays over Wat Kalayanamit light up the river.

Tip: The show schedules are quite close together, so visit on separate days to enjoy both without rushing. From the Wat Pho Pier only the left side of the fireworks is visible, which shows just part of the display.

Thai costume photos, for anyone interested

Many visitors dress in Thai costumes and take photos with Wat Arun as the backdrop. For anyone interested, the photos come out well with the temple behind, especially in the golden hour before the drone show starts. Costume rental and photographers are available at the shops near Tha Tien.

Roadside photo spots near the pier also frame the temple without any costume at all. A night photo spot on Soi Tha Suphan puts Wat Arun in the background, and the chedi of Wat Pho glows at night nearby.

Where to watch: the pier or a rooftop

Many rooftop restaurants and bars in the area offer a bird's-eye view of Wat Arun and the river.

Rooftops and boats offer other views.

  • Food and drink prices on the rooftops are a bit high.
  • Many front-row rooftop tables are reserved and crowded.
  • The rooftops felt cramped, with limited space and many people. The Wat Pho Pier is a better bet for a free, open view.

Tips for a great evening

1. Arrive early

The drone show starts at 7:45 PM, but the crowd is usually full by 6:00 PM. Arriving early secures a good spot and leaves time for costume photos before the show.

2. Plan for separate days

The drone show and the fireworks are close together in the schedule. Visiting on separate days makes it easy to enjoy both without rushing.

3. Getting home

When the drone show ends at the Wat Pho Pier, everyone heads home. Some walk to Memorial Bridge for the fireworks, while others wait at the pier. Even with so many people, the walk out is quick: about 500 meters back to Sanam Chai station, a smooth exit despite the crowd.

Final thoughts

The Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 festival is a fantastic free event that shows off the beauty of Thailand. Whether the draw is the drone show, the fireworks, or a Thai costume photo with Wat Arun behind, it makes for an unforgettable evening. Vijit Chao Phraya 2026: questions

What is the Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 festival?

A free riverside festival in Bangkok, from 13 November to 13 December 2026, with a drone show above Wat Arun, fireworks near Memorial Bridge and events along the Chao Phraya River.

Is the Wat Arun drone show free?

Yes. The festival and both shows are free to attend; Wat Pho Pier opposite Wat Arun is a free, open viewing spot.

What time do the drone show and the fireworks start?

The drone show starts at 7:45 PM and the fireworks start at 8:45 PM near Memorial Bridge. Check the official schedule poster for the show dates.

How do I get to the Wat Arun drone show by MRT?

Take the MRT to Sanam Chai station and use Exit 1, near Museum Siam. Walk around to the back entrance of the museum, turn right and continue straight for about 500 meters to reach the Wat Pho Pier opposite Wat Arun.

Where is the best place to watch the drone show?

The Wat Pho Pier directly opposite Wat Arun is the prime viewing location. Rooftop restaurants and bars give a bird's-eye view, but prices are a bit high, front-row tables are often reserved and the space is crowded.

Can I watch both the drone show and the fireworks on the same night?

The two schedules are close together, so visiting on separate days is easier. From the Wat Pho Pier only the left side of the fireworks is visible, which shows only part of the display.

How early should I arrive?

The drone show starts at 7:45 PM, but the crowd is usually full by about 6:00 PM, so arrive early for a good spot and time for costume photos.

How much does a Thai costume photo cost near Wat Arun?

At the local shops near Tha Tien, costume rental was 200฿ for men and 450฿ for women, and a photographer was 1,500฿ for 1 hour.

How do I get home after the show?

Walk about 500 meters back to Sanam Chai MRT station. The exit was smooth even with the crowds.
