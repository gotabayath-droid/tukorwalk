About the event: Queen Suriyothai at Wat Phra Ram

The Ayutthaya Light and Sound Show: Queen Suriyothai Experience was staged at Wat Phra Ram, inside Ayutthaya Historical Park, on 6–8 November 2026 as part of the Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair, a Thai tourism and heritage event. It doubled as a 25th-anniversary tribute to the iconic Suriyothai film, retelling the story of Queen Suriyothai on a stage built among the ruins.

The day worked in two halves: a morning at other sights around Ayutthaya, then the evening at the event. Entry to the show and to the food festival beside it was completely free, and most of the audience was Thai, with very few foreign visitors. That made it a genuinely local cultural night rather than a tourist show.

Light and Sound drama: the experience

The stage, ruins and lighting effects

The stage stands in front of the illuminated prang of Wat Phra Ram, so the ruins themselves are the backdrop. Lighting and smoke shift the mood from scene to scene, with red-lit battles, blue and green washes, and giant projections, including a Buddha image, cast over the old brickwork. Presenters open the evening.

The queen's story: processions and reverence

Royal processions cross the stage with palanquins and ceremonial umbrellas, the king appears on a palanquin against a projection, and Queen Suriyothai's own procession follows. Quieter scenes of reverence, with performers kneeling, sit between the battles.

Live action: war elephants, horses and pyrotechnics

The spectacle is real, not projected: war elephants carrying warriors walk before the illuminated prang, a female warrior rides one of them, and horseback battles and duels sweep across the stage. Sword fights break out through smoke, and the finale brings fireworks and confetti.

Traditional Khon dance and cultural performances

Between the battle scenes, traditional Khon-style dance and Thai cultural performances add a quieter layer, including performers in demon costume and Thai dancers on stage.

Finale and curtain call

The drama ends with a grand finale on stage, a curtain call for the performers, a lineup of the main cast, and a group photo of the cast with the elephants.

Seating, timing and facilities

The show is free and starts at 7 PM. The organizers arrange about 1,000 seats, but because so many Thai locals attend, the seating fills early. Arrive at least 1 hour before the show, otherwise there may be no seat. The drama runs about 45 minutes.

  • Toilets: there are no permanent washrooms close by; the organizers provide mobile restroom trailers for men and women.
  • Alcohol: the area is totally alcohol-free.
  • Getting back: few taxis run in the area at night, so arrange transport to the hotel in advance.
  • Food: the food festival is on the other side of the grounds (see the next part).
Tip: Go early, sit close, and keep the food festival for after the show: it stays open and busy once the drama ends.

Planning ahead: Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair 2027

Anyone who missed this edition can plan the next one. The Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair returns as Yoyos Ying Fah (Celebrating the Glory of Ayutthaya, a World Heritage Site), 8–17 January 2027, a larger festival in Ayutthaya Historical Park. A follow-up from that event will be posted here.

Ayutthaya Light and Sound Show: questions

What is the Ayutthaya Light and Sound Show?

A free night drama staged among the ruins of Wat Phra Ram in Ayutthaya Historical Park. The Queen Suriyothai Experience retells the queen's story with war elephants, horses, Khon dance, projections and fireworks, and it was a 25th-anniversary tribute to the Suriyothai film.

When and where was the Queen Suriyothai event held?

On 6–8 November 2026 at Wat Phra Ram, Ayutthaya Historical Park, as part of the Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair.

Is the Ayutthaya Light and Sound Show free?

Yes. The show and the food festival beside it were completely free to enter.

What time does the show start and how early should I arrive?

The show starts at 7 PM. About 1,000 seats are provided and they fill with Thai locals, so arrive at least 1 hour early or there may be no seat.

How long is the show?

About 45 minutes. A live music show with seating at bamboo tables follows beside the food festival.

Are there toilets at the event?

Yes. The organizers provide mobile restroom trailers for men and women.

Is alcohol allowed at the Ayutthaya night festival?

No. The area is totally alcohol-free.

How do I get back to my hotel at night?

Arrange transport in advance: there are few taxis in the area at night.

Are many foreign tourists there?

Very few. Most of the audience is Thai, which makes it a local cultural experience.

When is the next Ayutthaya Historical Park event?

Yoyos Ying Fah (Celebrating the Glory of Ayutthaya, a World Heritage Site) runs 8–17 January 2027, the Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair 2027.
