Ayutthaya Food Festival
authentic, cheap Thai street food around the illuminated ruins

Beside the Light and Sound Show, a free night food market of grilled skewers, sizzling seafood, spring rolls and Thai sweets, served by vendors in traditional dress, with live music and seating at bamboo tables.

✓ Free to enter✓ Very cheap, authentic Thai food✓ Alcohol-free

Quick answer

Beside the Ayutthaya Light and Sound Show, the festival grounds around Wat Phra Ram hold a free night food market: authentic Thai street food at very cheap prices, vendors in traditional Thai dress, live music and seating at bamboo tables. It is quite different from the stalls in Bangkok or Ayutthaya town, the people are very friendly, and the whole area is alcohol-free.

Night market and atmosphere around Wat Phra Ram

On the other side of the grounds from the stage, the festival turns into a food market. Rows of stalls run under the lights beside the illuminated temple grounds, and the whole place feels relaxed: families walk the aisles, vendors call out, and the food is really tasty, with many authentic Thai dishes at very cheap prices. It is totally different from the usual food stalls and vendors in Bangkok or in Ayutthaya town.

Many vendors are dressed in traditional Thai dress, which adds to the atmosphere and makes for easy, friendly photos. The people running the stalls are very friendly, and the market stays busy after the Light and Sound drama ends.

Grills and skewers

Charcoal and grill smoke mark the best corners: meat and vegetable skewers, garlic-and-chili skewers, grilled squid, and a sizzling seafood clay pot served hot at the table.

Snacks, sweets and drinks

Away from the grills, there are crisp fried spring rolls in bamboo baskets, trays of colorful Thai desserts, flower-shaped sweets and cold drinks from large dispenser jars. Everything is easy to eat while walking.

Live music and bamboo tables

After the drama, a live music show starts beside the food stalls, with seating at tables made of bamboo. It is a pleasant place to eat with the music playing, and a good reason to stay for the rest of the evening.

Practical notes

  • Free entry: both the show and the food festival cost nothing to enter; only the food is paid for, and it is cheap.
  • Alcohol-free: the area is totally alcohol-free.
  • Getting back: few taxis run at night, so arrange transport to the hotel before the evening ends.
  • Timing: see the show first (arrive 1 hour early for a seat), then eat and listen to the live music.
  • Toilets: the organizers provide mobile restroom trailers for men and women.

Ayutthaya food festival: questions

What food is at the Ayutthaya night festival?

Authentic Thai street food: grilled meat, vegetable and squid skewers, grilled eggplant, sizzling seafood in clay pots, fried spring rolls, and Thai desserts and sweets.

Is the food at the Ayutthaya festival cheap?

Yes. The food was tasty, authentic and very cheap, and quite different from the stalls and vendors in Bangkok or in Ayutthaya town.

Is there live music and seating?

Yes. A live music show follows the drama, with seating at tables made of bamboo beside the food stalls.

Is alcohol sold at the food festival?

No. The whole area is alcohol-free.

How do I get back after the festival?

Arrange transport to the accommodation in advance, since few taxis run at night.

Is the Ayutthaya food festival only during the Light and Sound Show?

It ran alongside the show on 6–8 November 2026, and a larger festival, the Ayutthaya World Heritage Fair 2027, is planned for 8–17 January 2027.
